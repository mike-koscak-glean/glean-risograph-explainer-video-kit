# Example: General Glean Context Explainer

## Audience
Business and technical audiences who know Glean as search but do not yet understand the shared context layer.

## Job of the video
Explain why connected, permission-aware context exists before a user asks a question, and why that foundation supports answers, recommendations, and agents.

## Core idea
Glean is not searching from scratch; it has already connected the company’s systems, indexed permitted content, and linked entities in a knowledge graph.

## Story shape
- Opening: one question appears to require searching many systems.
- Current state: the useful pieces live in documents, conversations, tickets, and records.
- Model: connectors feed an index; permissions remain intact; entities become a graph.
- Example: a forecast question expands through deals, calls, support cases, customers, and product issues.
- Implication: the same foundation supports more than one question.
- Close: the foundation is built before anyone asks.

## Duration
Approximately 3–4 minutes.

## Tone
Warm, confident, curious, and plainspoken.

## Evidence
Product architecture and examples must be checked against current approved Glean materials.
