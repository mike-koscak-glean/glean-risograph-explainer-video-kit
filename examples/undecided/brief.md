# Example: Project Type Undecided

## Starting point
Create a video about how teams can use company context to make AI assistance more useful. The strongest audience and framing are not yet clear.

## Context and research
- Approach: Search Glean first, then combine the findings with any supplied notes.
- Search focus: current internal explanations, recurring customer questions, enablement material, and recent examples.
- Time frame: prioritize current and recently updated sources.
- Exclude: confidential customer details and unsupported performance claims.

## Freeform notes
Could be a broad educational video for business audiences, or a customer-specific story if one account provides a particularly strong and approved example. Keep the explanation simple. Avoid a feature tour. The main idea is that useful assistance depends on connected, permission-aware context.

## Desired outcome
After research, recommend whether the video should be a general explainer or customer-specific. Present the tradeoff before drafting the full story.
